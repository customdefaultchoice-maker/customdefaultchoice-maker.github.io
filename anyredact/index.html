AnyRedact

Free browser-based AI redaction tool for PDFs, documents, spreadsheets, images, and audio.

AnyRedact detects and removes personal and sensitive data across PDFs, images, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, plain text, and audio directly in the browser without storing files.

  • Broad Format Support: PDFs, scanned images with OCR, Word (DOCX), PowerPoint (PPTX), Excel (XLSX/ODS), plain text, and audio.
  • Local Document Reconstruction in the browser.
  • AI PII detection with human review before export.
  • Zero storage architecture.
